We're Hiring Servers!

Toribashi is looking for attentive and reliable servers to join our team on Alvarado Street in downtown Monterey 🍜

Our dining room can move quickly through lunch and dinner service, so we are looking for servers who remain calm under a rush, know the menu well enough to guide guests through it, and take good care of every table. Prior serving experience is a plus, but a good attitude and a willingness to learn matter most. If this sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you!

How to Apply

Email your resume to toribashi.food [AT] gmail.com (replace [AT] with @), and be sure to include your phone number. For your email, use the following subject line:

Server Application — [NAME]

Instructions

  • Please replace [NAME] in the subject with your full name.
  • In addition to your attached resume, please provide the EXACT days (Mon, Tues, etc.) and the hours you are available to work.
  • Provide two non-familial references from different work experiences (reachable by both phone and email) who can speak to your reliability and professionalism.

Failure to comply with any of the instructions above or showcasing dishonesty in your application, such as providing inaccurate hours and/or inadequate references, may result in your application not being reviewed, rescinding of offer, or termination of employment.
