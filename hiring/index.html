We're Hiring!

Kafe Toribashi is looking for passionate, friendly, and reliable full-time staff to join us on Fisherman's Wharf 🎣

We strive to create a cozy, enjoyable environment and serve tasty and quality treats for our customers. Our goal is to expand our menu in the future, and we are looking for staff who are eager to learn and grow with us. If this sounds exciting, we'd love to hear from you!

How to Apply

Email your resume to kafe.toribashi [AT] gmail.com (replace [AT] with @), and be sure to include your phone number. For your email, use the following subject line:

Application — [NAME]

Instructions

  • Please replace [NAME] in the subject with your full name.
  • In addition to your attached resume, please provide the EXACT days (Mon, Tues, etc.) and the hours you are available to work.
  • Provide two non-familial references from different work experiences (reachable by both phone and email) who can speak to your reliability and professionalism.

Failure to comply with any of the instructions above or dishonesty in your application, such as providing inaccurate hours and/or inadequate references, may result in your application not being reviewed or immediate termination of employment.
